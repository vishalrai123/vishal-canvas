# Surface brief: home and inner pages

Mode: Experience. The work leads; the interface recedes after the home page.
Audience: recruiters and hiring managers skimming; engineering leads reading case studies for depth.
Constraints: static export, no stock photos, no invented claims, no employer or product names, motion off under reduced-motion. Direction pinned by the owner: dark cinematic.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A screening, not a brochure: 21 projects cut like a film, with a calm readable reel for skimmers. Refuses the category default of a centred headline over a grid of equal cards.
OWN-WORLD: Teal-black grade ground, warm sand text, one vermilion accent reserved for the primary action and focus. Outfit display in extreme weight contrast, Geist for reading. Film grain from an SVG noise layer, sand hairlines, and stacked plates as the component language. Recognisable with all content removed.
STORY: In one viewport the visitor learns who Vishal Rai is and what kind of problems are solved, believes it through quantified outcomes from the task files, and opens a case study or the resume.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating solid pill nav. H1 offset left at max-w-6xl in two to three lines with two inline SVG artifacts, a one-line role beneath, two CTAs with the primary at left. A fan of three overlapping plates, built from real featured task titles, rises from bottom right and overlaps the headline's last line.
FORM: Artistic Asymmetry hero, horizontal accordion slices by discipline, pinned-title split of quantified outcomes, highlights carousel; seed: none (unavailable) because the owner pinned the direction.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
